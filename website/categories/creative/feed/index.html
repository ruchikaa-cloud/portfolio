INTERSECT

(Description)

Intersect is a tabloid created for creatives navigating or preparing to enter the world of business. Positioned at the intersection of art and commerce, it explores the ideas, industries and current affairs that shape the professional world around creative thinkers. Through relevant stories and real-world examples, Intersect makes fields such as business and economics more accessible, helping creatives build the knowledge and perspective needed to thrive in an evolving industry.
Written. Edited. Shot. By me.

Challenges & Objectives

/ Project Overview

INTERSECT is a creative business tabloid designed around the intersection of art, culture, creativity, and commerce. The publication is created for young creatives, designers, entrepreneurs, and professionals who want to understand the business world without losing their creative perspective.

The tabloid explores subjects such as business, economics, branding, markets, trends, entrepreneurship, and current affairs, translating complex ideas into visually engaging and accessible stories. Instead of treating business as a separate discipline, INTERSECT looks at how it influences and is influenced by the creative industries.

/ Challenges

The main challenge was balancing creative visual expression with business-focused content while keeping the information clear and engaging. I also had to simplify complex topics, maintain a consistent visual identity, and create layouts that felt experimental yet readable, helping INTERSECT stand apart from a conventional business publication.

 
 

/ Objectives

The project combines editorial storytelling with experimental visual design, using bold typography, collage, photography, grids, handwritten elements, and contrasting colours to create a contemporary tabloid identity.

ethereal echoes

This photobook explores the mystery and majesty of nature, capturing its vast landscapes, hidden details, and moments of human connection. Each section focuses on a unique aspect of the natural world and our interactions with it, revealing nature’s power, beauty, and fragility.

Challenges
& Objectives

/ Project Overview

Ethereal Echoes is a photography photobook that explores the mystery, beauty, and emotional presence of the natural world. The project brings together street photography, fine art photography, and landscape photography, transforming everyday observations and fleeting moments into a cohesive visual narrative. Through composition, light, texture, and atmosphere, the photobook captures both the grandeur of landscapes and the subtle details often overlooked in everyday life.

/ Challenges

One of the main challenges was creating a consistent visual language across different photography styles. Balancing street, fine art, and landscape imagery while maintaining a cohesive narrative required careful selection, sequencing, editing, and experimentation with composition and colour. Another challenge was capturing spontaneous moments while ensuring that each image contributed meaningfully to the overall story.

/ Objectives

The objective of Ethereal Echoes was to explore photography as a form of visual storytelling, portraying nature and everyday surroundings through an artistic and atmospheric lens. The project aims to encourage viewers to pause and notice the beauty, emotion, textures, and fleeting moments that exist within both natural and urban environments.

/ Final Outcome

The final product was a visually captivating and functionally efficient design that not only met but exceeded client expectations. The revamped brand identity received positive feedback from users and increased engagement rates by 67%.

Challenges & Objectives

/ Project Overview

Eternum is a wedding magazine that explores weddings beyond their visual grandeur. It focuses on the emotions, relationships, cultural traditions, and shared experiences that make each celebration meaningful. Divided into five slugs Decor, Destination, Ceremony, Heritage, and Food the magazine brings together stories of love, family, friendship, nostalgia, and tradition through an intimate editorial approach.

/ Challenges

One of the key challenges was creating a magazine that felt personal and emotional while still maintaining a refined editorial aesthetic. Balancing diverse elements such as décor, food, heritage, destinations, and ceremonies without making the magazine feel disconnected required a strong visual and content direction. Another challenge was representing the richness of wedding traditions in a contemporary way while preserving their cultural and emotional significance.

/ Objectives

The objective of Eternum is to present weddings as more than just celebrations between two people. It aims to document the cultural, emotional, and social layers surrounding weddings while highlighting the details, traditions, people, and memories that connect generations. Through visual storytelling and editorial content, Eternum creates a space where contemporary wedding culture meets heritage and personal narratives.
