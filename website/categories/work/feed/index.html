Working as a Shoot Production Assistant for Kushal’s Fashion Jewellery, I gained hands-on experience in the planning and execution of a brand photoshoot. I assisted the creative and production teams with shoot coordination, styling support, prop and product arrangements, and on-set organization. This experience helped me understand how creative concepts are translated into visual campaigns while ensuring that every element stays aligned with the brand’s aesthetic, campaign requirements, and production timelines.

During the shoot, I assisted with shoot coordination, styling, jewellery placement, product arrangement, and on-set organization. I also assisted with last-minute adjustments, coordinated between the creative and production requirements, and helped maintain visual consistency with the brand’s aesthetic throughout the shoot. This experience gave me hands-on exposure to the production process and how a creative concept is translated into final campaign visuals.

I explored floor planning and store layouts, observed customer journeys, and learned how visual merchandising, product placement, and attention to detail influence the way customers interact with a space. 

 

The internship allowed me to see how a brand’s identity can extend beyond its products into the physical environment, creating a cohesive and immersive experience. Retail Management Intern | Nicobar

Assisted with floor planning and store layout to create a seamless customer experience. Observed customer journeys and buying behaviour to understand how people interact with the space. Supported visual merchandising, including product placement and display presentation. Monitored day-to-day store operations and maintained visual and organizational standards. Scoop Publicists

My time as a PR Intern has been an incredible learning experience. From media and influencer outreach, media mapping and coverage tracking to working on client reports and brand communication, I got to experience the world of PR firsthand.

This internship has not only helped me grow professionally but also made me realise how much I genuinely enjoy and am passionate about Public Relations.

I also had the opportunity to work with some incredible brands, including Maverick & Farmer, Shangri-La Bengaluru, Chinita, Tagda Raho, and Watsons. Working across such diverse brands gave me valuable exposure to different aspects of PR and made the entire experience even more exciting and insightful.

So grateful to the entire Scoop Publicists team for all the guidance, learning, and wonderful memories.

Brands I Worked With

During my time at Scoop Publicists, I had the opportunity to work across a diverse portfolio of brands, including Shangri-La Bengaluru, Magnolia Bakery, Chinita, Tagda Raho, Watsons, and Maverick & Farmer. From hospitality and food to lifestyle and entertainment, working across these different brands gave me the opportunity to understand how PR strategies, media outreach, influencer relations, and brand communication can take different forms for every client. Each brand brought a new perspective, a new challenge, and a new learning experience that made my journey at Scoop even more meaningful.
