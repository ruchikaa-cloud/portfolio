Reality TV has always been in guilty pleasure for the audience worldwide. It’s an escape for us to see an unscripted life of someone which has raw emotion and goes deep into their personal life as people. Behind all the fame that comes with it and the fans, is its dark truth with lies which consists of psychological distress exploitation and some tragic concerns and struggle that the people face.

This is not really spoken about because until an incident happens nobody really cares about what goes on BTS! So, let’s deep dive into this long Tunnel with too many concealed truths.

Starting out with the contestants, reality TV make the contestants sign contracts willingly. Now a lot of us don’t ready understand to what extent their commitment is and what the consequences could be in the aftermath but the truth is even with all the financial stability they find themselves at the mercy of the producers who only care about their ratings instead of the people behind the making.

Exploitation

Producers, makers manipulate contestants and situations to create drama, contestants often go to the shows for the success but they find themselves in a loop where they are forced into participating until the breakdown, this leads to immense emotional stress and isolation from the real world. To make the matters worse the people who make these series use their contestant’s distress and evolve it more into to a bigger drama, they link their real life to the reality TV live to make it more intense for a better Audience experience.

They struggle with breakdowns and they are never looked after. It’s becoming very alarming, where Day after day we get to see the cases increase of suicide, depression and anxiety.

The ‘IMAGE’

When we look at TV reality contestants, we always tend to look at them with a particular narrative. This usually happens because it’s a perception that we have created by looking at the reality TV, which is not really true but it’s portrayed in such a way. This sounds like a small problem but the truth is the people’s life who are in the reality TV completely changes because in the professional and personal life they are taken in the same portrayed way, instead of who they really are! They have reported facing online trolling, criticism and death threats because of the way they have behaved on the show. Audiences don’t care about who they are or what their life is all about except for their 15 minutes of fame on the show.

Psychological distress

I cannot emphasize on this more but when you are in the public eye after a point of time it does get overwhelming especially for the people who have been in this industry for a long time. Reality TV contestants often experience intense reactions from the audience when it comes to what they have to say about the person it leads to a lot of anxiety and some stress disorders, which takes them years and years to overcome. Producers and makers of these series use unethical production practices which lead the contestants who have a men’s pressure.

There have been so many contestants who have died by suicide after doing certain reality TV shows and this is all because people forget what is real and what is scripted.

The audience

Believe it or not, but the truth remains that there is a big effect on the society and on us as the audience. We look at these TV shows and promote it in unrealistic ways because we tend to look at them as a sensitive escape for our satisfaction. TV shows promote unrealistic beauty standards and toxic relationships, for example dating reality shows tend to reinforce physical appearance rather than meaningful connections.

People have normalised bullying and toxic behaviour by the audience and viewers as they see it as an idea to be real through the show. The truth is the producers are the makers of the shows are never taken accountable there is always a loop hole in the industry which shows the reason after numerous controversies and deaths they will never be accountability taken because they have the power in the industry. Contestants have strict contracts where they are not able to speak up about what they went through and the violation that have occurred during their times.

We, the audience can actually help them and there is a way forward to go about it that the dark side of the TV cannot be ignored, there has to be a sensitive improvement which can happen if there are stick to regulations to protect the people from exploitation and mental harm. We need to speak out about what is wrong and what is right instead of only criticizing the contestants for what they have done. Additionally, it’s not the TV reality has always been in this phase but the current trends and practices have led to a greater downfall for the people working in it. We are need to take a step forward to reinforce the past and make it a safe space for everybody.
